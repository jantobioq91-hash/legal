Privacy Policy

App: Vigia Megapack · Responsible party: Jose Antonio Aguila Quispe (individual, Lima, Peru) · Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. What this app is

Vigia Megapack is an internal tool. Its only user is its owner, who uses it to manage his own Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ad accounts: creating and pausing ads, reviewing results and receiving alerts. The app is not open to the public and has no external users.

2. What data it processes

Through Meta's Marketing API, the app only accesses data from the owner's own ad accounts, pages and pixels: campaigns, ad sets, ads, budgets and aggregated performance metrics (spend, impressions, clicks, purchases). It does not collect personal data from other people, does not ask third parties to log in, and does not access anyone's profiles, messages or contact lists.

3. How it is used

Only so the owner can manage and measure his own advertising. Data is never sold, rented or shared with third parties, and is not used to build profiles of people.

4. Storage and retention

Data is read from Meta when needed and, if stored, stays on the owner's computer, protected by his user account. Access credentials are kept only on that computer, in his user account settings, and never in public files or repositories. Data that is no longer needed is deleted.

5. How to request deletion of your data

If you believe this app holds any data about you and you want it deleted, email Geminis_antonio15@hotmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We will confirm receipt and delete what applies within 30 days. You can also remove the app's access from your Facebook account settings, under "Apps and websites".

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with its date.
